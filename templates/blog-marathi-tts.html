This is everything a Marathi content creator or educator needs to know.

Why Marathi TTS Has Been Difficult

Marathi shares the Devanagari script with Hindi but has several distinct phonological features that make it uniquely challenging for TTS systems:

  • Unique retroflex lateral ळ — This sound is central to Marathi but absent from Hindi. Old TTS engines would mispronounce every word containing this character.
  • Distinct vowel patterns — Marathi has vowel sounds and schwa deletion rules that differ from Hindi, requiring language-specific training data.
  • Conjunct consonants — Marathi uses conjunct consonants in patterns that differ from Hindi, particularly in word endings.
  • Regional dialect variation — Pune Marathi (standard), Nagpur Marathi, and Konkan Marathi have distinct pronunciation patterns.
  • Anusvara usage — Marathi uses the anusvara (ं) in different contexts than Hindi, requiring Marathi-specific training.

Old TTS engines defaulted to Hindi phonology for Marathi Devanagari text — producing an accent that sounded completely foreign to native Marathi speakers. Neural TTS trained specifically on Marathi speaker data handles all of these correctly.

How to Write Marathi Text for Best TTS Output

  • Always use proper matras and vowel marks. "कम" and "काम" are completely different words — never skip diacritics.
  • Use the danda (।) for sentence endings. TTS engines need punctuation to know where to pause naturally.
  • Write numbers in Marathi words. "पाच" sounds more natural than "5" in Marathi TTS.
  • Use standard Marathi orthography. Some words are spelled differently in Marathi vs Hindi even in Devanagari — use Marathi spelling.
  • Avoid Hindi spellings for Marathi words. Always write from a native Marathi speaker's perspective.
  • Test regional vocabulary first. Words from Pune vs Nagpur vs Konkan Marathi may vary — always test before full generation.

🟠 Key Marathi Writing Tips:

• Retroflex lateral ळ — This is the most critical Marathi-specific sound. Use it correctly in words like "वळण", "जळगाव", "कळणे"

• Aspirated consonants ख, घ, छ, झ, ठ, ढ, थ, ध, फ, भ — Write these correctly, not their non-aspirated equivalents

• Chandrabindu (ँ) — Include for nasalised vowels specific to Marathi pronunciation

• न vs ण — Distinguish correctly based on Marathi word etymology for natural pronunciation
