Complete Podcast Production Workflow

1

Write a Podcast-Optimised Script

Writing for podcast TTS narration requires a conversational tone that sounds natural when spoken:

  • Write like you speak. Use contractions: "you're" not "you are", "it's" not "it is". Formal written language sounds stiff when converted to speech.
  • Short sentences for energy. Vary sentence length — mix short punchy sentences with longer ones to create natural rhythm.
  • Use ellipses for dramatic pauses. "And then... something changed." creates tension in storytelling podcasts.
  • Write out numbers and symbols. "Forty-five percent" not "45%". "Twenty twenty-six" not "2026".
  • Add stage directions as comments. Write "[pause]" or "[slow down]" as reminders — then remove before generating, adjusting your text punctuation instead.
  • Keep episodes under 5,000 characters per section. Split at natural break points — chapter endings, topic transitions.

3

Generate and Edit Audio

  • Generate each section as a separate MP3 file
  • Import all sections into Audacity (free) or GarageBand
  • Add intro music at -20dB below the voice track
  • Normalise the full episode to -16 LUFS (Spotify's recommended loudness for podcasts)
  • Export as MP3 at 192kbps, 44.1kHz stereo — the standard for podcast platforms
  • Add ID3 metadata: episode title, podcast name, episode number, cover art
4

Upload to Podcast Platforms

Use a free podcast host like Anchor (Spotify for Podcasters) to distribute to all major platforms simultaneously. Upload your MP3, add episode title, description, and cover art, and your episode goes live on Spotify, Apple Podcasts, JioSaavn, and Google Podcasts automatically.

💡 Loudness Tip: Use Audacity's Effect → Loudness Normalisation tool and set to -16 LUFS for Spotify/Apple Podcasts, or -14 LUFS for YouTube. This prevents your podcast from sounding quieter than others on the same platform — one of the most common beginner mistakes.

Monetising an AI TTS Podcast

  • Spotify Audience Network — Once you have 100+ followers and 1,000+ streams in 60 days, apply for Spotify's podcast advertising program.
  • YouTube Monetisation — Upload episodes as YouTube videos. Once you hit 1,000 subscribers and 4,000 watch hours, enable AdSense monetisation.
  • Sponsorships — With 500+ regular listeners, approach brands in your niche for direct sponsorship deals. AI-generated ad reads mean you can personalise sponsor segments for each episode at zero additional cost.
  • Patreon / Subscription — Offer bonus episodes or early access to paying subscribers on Patreon or YouTube Membership.
  • Affiliate Marketing — Mention affiliate products naturally in your narration. Include affiliate links in episode descriptions.

✅ AI Podcast Disclosure: Spotify and Apple Podcasts currently do not require disclosure of AI-generated voices. However, being transparent with your audience builds long-term trust. Many successful AI podcast creators mention their production method openly — listeners appreciate the honesty and it does not reduce listenership.
