What is Text to Speech?

Text to Speech (TTS) is a powerful AI technology that converts written text into natural human-like voice. It is widely used in modern content creation.

Earlier systems sounded robotic, but modern AI voices are realistic and expressive.

TTS is used in YouTube videos, audiobooks, podcasts, and accessibility tools.

It helps people with disabilities consume content easily.

Businesses use TTS for automation and customer support systems.

Overall, it saves time, reduces cost, and increases productivity.
