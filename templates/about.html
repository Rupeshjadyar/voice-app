About VoicePro

VoicePro TTS Studio is a free, browser-based AI text-to-speech platform built to make professional-quality voice generation accessible to everyone — content creators, educators, developers, and small businesses — without subscriptions, accounts, or software to install.

Our Story

VoicePro started with a simple frustration: creating audio content in Indian regional languages was needlessly expensive and complicated. Quality TTS tools required API accounts, billing setup, and technical knowledge. Free alternatives sounded robotic and mispronounced basic Hindi and Marathi words.

We built VoicePro to solve this. Our goal was a tool that a teacher in a small town, a YouTuber just starting out, or an NGO building accessible content could open in a browser and immediately generate professional-quality audio — in Hindi, Marathi, Tamil, or any other language they needed — with no barriers.

We launched in late 2025 and have been improving the platform continuously based on feedback from creators, educators, and developers who use it every day.

What VoicePro Does

VoicePro converts typed or pasted text into natural-sounding MP3 or WAV audio files using AI neural voice technology. Users can choose from 100+ languages, select from 8 different voice characters, adjust speaking speed and pitch, and apply speaking styles such as Newscast, Documentary, Cheerful, or Poetry Reading.

Audio is generated in real time — typically within 2–5 seconds — and can be downloaded immediately as MP3 (compressed, universally compatible) or WAV (uncompressed, studio quality). No account is needed, no personal data is stored, and there are no usage limits beyond the per-request character limit.

Our Technology

VoicePro uses Microsoft's Edge Neural TTS engine — the same neural voice infrastructure behind Microsoft Azure Cognitive Services. This gives the platform access to hundreds of neural voices trained on native speaker recordings across more than 100 languages, including dedicated high-quality models for all major Indian regional languages.

Each request is processed in real time. Text is analysed for context, prosody, and intonation before audio is synthesised — this is what produces natural-sounding speech rather than robotic word-by-word reading. Audio is returned as base64-encoded MP3 or WAV and delivered directly to your browser. Your text is never stored on our servers or used for any purpose beyond generating the requested audio.

How VoicePro Has Grown

August 2025 — First Version Launched

Initial release with basic TTS functionality, 5 languages, and MP3 download. Built to solve the problem of expensive and complicated Hindi TTS tools.

October 2025 — Indian Language Expansion

Added dedicated neural voices for all major Indian regional languages — Marathi, Tamil, Telugu, Gujarati, Kannada, Malayalam, Bengali, Punjabi, Odia, and Urdu — with native speaker-quality pronunciation.

December 2025 — Speaking Styles Added

Introduced 16 speaking styles including Documentary, Newscast, Cheerful, Poetry Reading, and Sports Commentary based on user feedback from content creators and educators.

February 2026 — WAV Download and Per-Voice Presets

Added WAV (uncompressed) export option and per-voice speed and pitch presets, making the tool more useful for professional audio production workflows.

2026 (Ongoing) — Continuous Improvement

Regular updates based on user feedback. Currently exploring additional Indian language voices, improved Hinglish handling, and SSML support for advanced users.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is VoicePro really free?
Yes, completely free. There are no premium plans, no subscription tiers, and no hidden charges. The tool will always be free to use. We do not upsell, require credit cards, or put generated audio behind a paywall.
How is VoicePro free if it uses paid APIs?
VoicePro uses the Microsoft Edge Neural TTS API, which provides a generous free usage tier. We optimise our implementation to stay within these free limits while delivering a reliable service to our users. If we ever exceed these limits significantly, we will look at sustainable ways to continue offering the service for free.
Is my text stored or used for training?
No. Text submitted to VoicePro is sent directly to the TTS engine for audio synthesis and is not stored on our servers. We do not log, analyse, or use your text content for any purpose. Generated audio is also deleted immediately after it is delivered to your browser.
Can I use generated audio commercially?
Yes. Audio generated using VoicePro is yours to use for personal or commercial purposes — YouTube videos, podcasts, e-learning courses, business presentations, and more. VoicePro claims no ownership over generated content and places no restrictions on its use. Note that commercial use of Microsoft Azure TTS in other contexts may be subject to Microsoft's own licensing terms.
How can I contact you or report a problem?
Use our Contact page to report bugs, suggest improvements, or ask questions. We read every message and aim to respond within 48 hours on working days.
