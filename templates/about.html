Our Technology

VoicePro is powered by Microsoft's Edge Neural TTS engine — the same infrastructure behind Microsoft Azure Cognitive Services. This gives us access to hundreds of neural voices trained on native speaker data across 100+ languages, including all major Indian regional languages.

Each voice generation request is processed in real time. Your text is sent to the neural engine, which analyses the context and produces audio with the correct prosody, rhythm, pauses, and intonation. The audio is returned as a base64-encoded MP3 or WAV and delivered instantly — your text is never stored on our servers.
