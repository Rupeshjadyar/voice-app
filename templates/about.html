About VoicePro

VoicePro TTS Studio is a free, browser-based AI text-to-speech platform built to make professional-quality voice generation accessible to everyone — content creators, educators, developers, and small businesses — without subscriptions, accounts, or software to install.

Our Story

VoicePro started with a simple frustration: creating audio content across international and regional languages—from American/European accents to Asian and Indian regional tongues—was needlessly expensive and complicated. Quality TTS tools required API accounts, billing setup, and technical knowledge. Free alternatives sounded robotic and mispronounced basic words across world languages.

We built VoicePro to solve this globally. Our goal was a tool that a teacher anywhere in the world, a YouTuber just starting out, or a developer building accessible content could open in a browser and immediately generate studio-grade audio — in English, Spanish, Hindi, Arabic, Japanese, or any of our 100+ supported languages — with zero barriers.

We launched in late 2025 and have been improving the platform continuously based on feedback from creators, educators, and developers worldwide who use it every day.

What VoicePro Does

VoicePro converts typed or pasted text into natural-sounding MP3 or WAV audio files using AI neural voice technology. Users worldwide can choose from 100+ global languages and country accents, select from diverse voice characters, adjust speaking speed and pitch, and apply speaking styles such as Newscast, Documentary, Cheerful, or Poetry Reading.

Audio is generated in real time — typically within 2–5 seconds — and can be downloaded immediately as MP3 (compressed, universally compatible) or WAV (uncompressed, studio quality). No account is needed, no personal data is stored, and there are no usage limits beyond the per-request character limit.

Our Technology

VoicePro uses high-end Neural TTS engines — the same neural voice infrastructure behind top cloud speech services. This gives the platform access to hundreds of neural voices trained on native speaker recordings across more than 100 languages across Europe, Americas, Asia, Middle East, Africa, and India.

Each request is processed in real time. Text is analysed for context, prosody, and intonation before audio is synthesised — this is what produces natural-sounding speech rather than robotic word-by-word reading. Audio is returned as base64-encoded MP3 or WAV and delivered directly to your browser. Your text is never stored on our servers or used for any purpose beyond generating the requested audio.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is VoicePro really free?
Yes, completely free. There are no premium plans, no subscription tiers, and no hidden charges. The tool will always be free to use. We do not upsell, require credit cards, or put generated audio behind a paywall.
How is VoicePro free if it uses paid APIs?
VoicePro uses the Microsoft Edge Neural TTS API, which provides a generous free usage tier. We optimise our implementation to stay within these free limits while delivering a reliable service to our users.
Is my text stored or used for training?
No. Text submitted to VoicePro is sent directly to the TTS engine for audio synthesis and is not stored on our servers. We do not log, analyse, or use your text content for any purpose.
Can I use generated audio commercially?
Yes. Audio generated using VoicePro is yours to use for personal or commercial purposes — YouTube videos, podcasts, e-learning courses, business presentations, and more. VoicePro claims no ownership over generated content.
How can I contact you or report a problem?
Use our Contact page to report bugs, suggest improvements, or ask questions. We read every message and aim to respond within 48 hours on working days.
