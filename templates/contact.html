Common Questions

Is VoicePro really free?

Yes, completely free for personal and commercial use. No hidden fees ever.

Why is my audio generation failing?

Check your internet connection and ensure the text is under 5,000 characters. Some languages may take slightly longer to generate.

Can I suggest a new language or voice?

Absolutely! Email us with your request. We actively work on expanding language coverage.

How do I report a bug?

Send us an email with a description of the issue, the language/voice you were using, and if possible a screenshot. We aim to fix bugs within 48 hours.

Or go back to generating voices — it's free!
