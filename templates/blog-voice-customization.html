Speed, Pitch & Speaking Style: Complete Voice Customisation Guide

April 1, 2026 7 min read VoicePro Team

The difference between a mediocre AI voiceover and a professional one often comes down to three settings: speed, pitch, and speaking style. Most creators use defaults and wonder why their audio sounds flat. This guide explains exactly what each control does and gives you the best settings for every content type.

The Three Core Controls Explained

⚡

Speed (Rate) — Controls Words Per Minute

Speed controls how fast the voice speaks. Default is 1.0x. Below 1.0x slows it down, above speeds it up.

SpeedEffectBest For
0.75x – 0.85xVery slow, deliberateMeditation, audiobooks, kids content
0.85x – 0.95xMeasured, seriousDocumentary, serious topics, Hindi educational
0.95x – 1.05xNatural conversationalTutorials, explainer videos, general use
1.1x – 1.2xEnergetic, engagingSocial media, gaming, news summaries
1.25x+Fast, punchyShort-form content, quick tips
🎵

Pitch — Controls Voice Frequency (High or Low)

Pitch controls how high or low the voice sounds. Negative = deeper, Positive = higher. Stay within -5 to +5 for professional results.

  • -5 to -3: Deep, bass resonance — documentary, authority content
  • -2 to -1: Slightly lower — news, finance, serious topics
  • 0 (Neutral): Voice's natural character — best for most content
  • +1 to +3: Brighter, approachable — educational, friendly content
  • +4 to +6: High energy — youth content, social media
  • Beyond ±7: Cartoon effect — comedy only
🔊

Volume — Keep at 100% Always

Always keep volume at 100% and adjust loudness in your video editor. Going below 70% introduces noise floor issues. Use Audacity's Loudness Normalisation to -14 LUFS for YouTube.

💡 Pro Tip: Always generate a 30-second test clip before committing to a full script. Change one setting at a time and listen to the difference. Making multiple changes at once makes it impossible to know what improved the audio.

✅ Golden Rule: When in doubt — Speed 1.0x, Pitch 0, Style "General". Then adjust one setting at a time from that clean baseline.
