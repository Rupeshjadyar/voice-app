How do I download the generated audio?

After generating, three download options appear: (1) Download as MP3 — standard compressed format, (2) Download as WAV — uncompressed studio quality, (3) Copy Audio Link — copy the base64 data URI to embed in your projects. All options are free and instant.

What is the character limit?

Up to 5,000 characters per request — about 700–800 words or 4–5 minutes of audio. No daily usage limit. For longer content, split into sections and merge the audio files.

Yes. Audio generated using VoicePro is yours to use freely — no copyright claims, no attribution required. Upload to YouTube, Instagram Reels, TikTok, Spotify podcasts, or any commercial project. VoicePro claims no ownership over generated content.
