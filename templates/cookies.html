Cookie Policy

Last Updated: August 2026 • VoicePro TTS Studio

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies and browser local storage are small text fragments stored on your device when you browse websites. They help websites remember your preferences, optimize user experience, provide analytical metrics, and serve relevant advertising.

2. Categories of Cookies We Use

Cookie Type Purpose Lifespan
Essential / Functional Stores UI theme preference (Dark/Light mode) and voice speed settings via HTML5 LocalStorage. Persistent (Local)
Analytics (Google Analytics 4) Collects anonymous aggregate usage statistics, pageviews, and error telemetry to optimize service speed. Up to 24 months
Advertising (Google AdSense) Third-party vendor cookies used by Google and partner networks to serve contextual ads that keep VoicePro 100% free. Configured by Google

3. How to Manage & Opt Out of Cookies

You can choose to disable, block, or delete cookies at any time through your web browser settings. Please note that disabling essential cookies may reset your theme preference upon page reload.

  • Google Ad Personalization: You can opt out of personalized ads by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Browser Controls: Check your browser's Help menu (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari) for instructions on clearing cookies.
