Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 January 2026

Summary (Plain English):
  • We do not collect your name, email, or personal data unless you contact us.
  • We do not store the text you submit for voice generation.
  • We use Google AdSense to show ads — this involves cookies from Google.
  • We use basic, anonymous analytics to understand how the site is used.
  • We do not sell your data to anyone, ever.

1. Who We Are

VoicePro TTS Studio ("VoicePro", "we", "our", "us") operates the website at texttoaudiomp3.site. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our free AI text-to-speech service.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

To be unambiguous about what we do not do:

  • We do not store the text you submit for voice generation. Text is processed by the TTS engine and immediately discarded.
  • We do not require account creation, and therefore collect no usernames or passwords.
  • We do not require email addresses to use the voice generation tool.
  • We do not store generated audio files on our servers after delivery to your browser.
  • We do not collect payment information of any kind (the service is free).

3. Information We Do Collect

3.1 Automatically Collected Data (Log Data)

When you visit our website, our web server automatically logs standard technical information including:

  • Your IP address (anonymised after 24 hours)
  • Browser type and version
  • Operating system
  • Referring URL (the page you came from)
  • Pages visited on our site and time of visit

This data is used solely for security monitoring, abuse prevention, and understanding aggregate usage patterns. It is not used to identify individual users.

3.2 Contact Form Data

If you submit a message via our Contact page, we collect the name and email address you provide, along with the content of your message. This information is used only to respond to your enquiry and is not used for marketing purposes.

3.3 Cookies

We use the following types of cookies:

  • Functional cookies: Small session cookies to remember your language or voice preferences within a single visit. These are deleted when you close your browser.
  • Analytics cookies: Anonymous usage analytics (if enabled) to understand how visitors use the site. No personally identifiable information is collected.
  • Advertising cookies: We use Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our site or other sites. See Google's Privacy Policy at policies.google.com/privacy for details.

You can control or delete cookies at any time through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect some site functionality.

4. Google AdSense and Advertising

VoicePro displays advertisements served by Google AdSense. Google uses the DoubleClick cookie to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to websites. You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

We do not control the content of Google-served advertisements. If you encounter an inappropriate advertisement, please use Google's ad feedback tool to report it.

5. Third-Party Services

VoicePro uses the following third-party services:

  • Microsoft Azure Cognitive Services / Edge TTS: Text submitted for voice generation is sent to Microsoft's TTS engine for processing. Microsoft's privacy policy governs this processing. See privacy.microsoft.com.
  • Google AdSense: For displaying advertisements. See Google's privacy policy above.
  • Google Fonts: For loading web fonts. Google may log requests for fonts. See Google's privacy policy.
  • Cloudflare CDN: For delivering Font Awesome icons and other static assets.

6. Data Retention

Text submitted for voice generation: Not stored (deleted immediately after audio synthesis).

Generated audio files: Not stored on our servers (delivered directly to your browser).

Server log data: Retained for up to 30 days for security purposes, then deleted.

Contact form messages: Retained for up to 12 months, then deleted.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights under applicable data protection law including:

  • The right to access personal data we hold about you
  • The right to correct inaccurate personal data
  • The right to request deletion of personal data
  • The right to object to processing of your personal data
  • The right to data portability

Because we collect minimal personal data, exercising most of these rights is straightforward. To make a request, contact us via our Contact page.

8. Children's Privacy

VoicePro is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected data from a child under 13, please contact us immediately and we will delete it.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date at the top. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of VoicePro after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or about how we handle your data, please contact us. We will respond within 48 hours on working days.
