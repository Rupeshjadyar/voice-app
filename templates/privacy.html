Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2026

🔒 The Short Version

VoicePro does not store your text, does not save your generated audio, does not require an account, and does not sell or share any personal data. Your privacy is protected by design.

1. Information We Collect

VoicePro is designed as a privacy-first service. We collect the absolute minimum necessary to operate:

  • Text input: Your text is transmitted to our TTS engine to generate audio. It is processed in memory only and never written to permanent storage.
  • Generated audio: Audio files are created on-demand and delivered to your browser. They are not saved on our servers. Once delivered, they are permanently deleted.
  • Anonymous usage statistics: We may collect aggregate, non-identifiable data such as the total number of requests per day and popular language choices. This data cannot be used to identify you.
  • Server logs: Standard web server logs (IP address, request timestamp, browser type) may be retained for up to 7 days for security and debugging purposes, then deleted.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect your name, email address, or any personal identifiers.
  • We do not create user accounts or profiles.
  • We do not track your activity across other websites.
  • We do not use third-party analytics services that track individual users.
  • We do not store your text content permanently.
  • We do not record or retain your generated audio files.

3. Cookies

VoicePro uses minimal cookies strictly necessary for the website to function correctly (such as session state). We do not use advertising cookies, tracking cookies, or third-party analytics cookies that identify individual users.

4. Third-Party Services

VoicePro uses the following third-party services:

  • Microsoft Azure / Edge Neural TTS: Text input is sent to Microsoft's TTS engine to generate audio. Microsoft's privacy policy applies to this processing. Text is used solely for audio generation and is not retained by Microsoft for this use case.
  • Google AdSense: We display advertisements served by Google AdSense. Google may use cookies to serve ads based on your browsing history. You can opt out via Google's ad settings.
  • Google Fonts: We load fonts from Google's CDN. This may transmit your IP address to Google's servers.

5. Your Rights

Because we do not collect personal data, there is no data about you to request, correct, or delete. If you have concerns about data processed by third-party services used on this site, please refer to their respective privacy policies. Contact us at rupeshjadyar21@gmail.com with any privacy questions.

6. Children's Privacy

VoicePro is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children. If you believe a child has used our service and you have concerns, contact us immediately.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our practices or applicable law. Significant changes will be noted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of VoicePro after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

8. Contact
